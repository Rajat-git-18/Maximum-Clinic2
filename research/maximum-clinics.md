## Omitted — needs confirmation

- Which phone number is the preferred or WhatsApp number. Both published numbers are shown as call links. WhatsApp links use those same numbers because they are the published contacts; a separate WhatsApp account was not confirmed.
- Consultation fee of ₹500 and “22 years in healthcare” from Justdial snippets.
- Doctor names, qualifications, portraits, registrations, and languages. None were on Instagram, Google, or Doczone.
- Whether the clinic is still part of Max Healthcare. The 2015 article is historical and is not stated on the current profiles.
- Parking, public transport, payment methods, first-visit checklist, and follow-up policy.
- Authentic interior or exterior photographs suitable to republish. Google has 16 photos; they were not downloaded or presented as site imagery.
- Patient reviews from other directories. Counts conflict with Google (other sites 67–235). Only the Google listing opened on 29 October 2026 is used: 4.7 from 88 reviews, and the first three review texts Google showed, unedited. The page links to the full Google list.
- The Instagram phrase “Best healthcare centre” is the clinic’s own bio line. It is not repeated as a claim on the website.
